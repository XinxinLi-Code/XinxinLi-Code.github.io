abstract: >-
  EditSR pairs a neural symbolic regression model with a pre-trained rectifier that repairs
  predicted expressions through successive, syntactically valid edits. Conditioning each edit
  on the current expression helps correct earlier errors and improves symbolic structure
  recovery, particularly for complex expressions, with limited additional computation.
