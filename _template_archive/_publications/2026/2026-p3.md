abstract: >-
    P3 addresses the variance and bias caused by single-sample latent estimates when training VAE-based robot policies with PPO. By propagating policy optimization over the latent distribution and calibrating behavior under uncertainty, it improves data efficiency, reduces convergence steps, and supports stable humanoid parkour learning.
