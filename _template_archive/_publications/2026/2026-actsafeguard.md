abstract: >-
  ActSafeGuard inserts a differentiable, training-aligned constraint layer into flow-matching VLA/WAM policies, enforcing hard per-step action feasibility during both learning and inference. Its analytical ray-scaling operator provides boundary-aware gradients and achieves 100% step safety while preserving or improving task success across pi0.5 and Fast-WAM backbones.
